feat(wallet): Rust wallet broadcasts a real tx — accepted by the Go chain

The standalone Rust wallet (crypto/src/bin/wallet.rs) now:

- derives wsrc's keys from recovery words (pubkey matches on-chain)
- fetches ring + encrypted balances + roothash + tip blid from the daemon
- builds a complete NORMAL transfer with the proven tx_builder
- self-verifies the range proof
- SUBMITS it to the Go simnode — the chain ACCEPTS it into the mempool
  (txid byte-identical to Go's own computation of the same blob)

Also fixed along the way:
- Witness.Balance = remaining (balance - value - fees - burn), not the
  full balance — the proof's range commitment was wrong before
- txid for the proof = get_hash(statement bpp), not hardcoded 3
- RingMember.balance_decoded (caller-supplied decrypted sender balance)
- simnode SubmitTX roundtrips serialize->deserialize (populates
  Publickeylist_pointers the mempool verify requires)
- ring members deduped by compressed key (the chain rejects duplicate
  pointers)

The tx is selected for mining by the simnode; block-execution re-verify
still drops it (Go's Proof.Verify rejects the Rust-generated proof — a
subtle bulletproof-generation divergence to chase next).
