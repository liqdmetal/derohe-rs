feat(wallet): mnemonic + key derivation — Rust reconstructs the simnode wallet

- mnemonics.rs: clean-room DERO recovery-words derivation (24 words →
  32-byte seed via the 3-words→4-bytes base-1626 mapping; 25th-word
  crc32 checksum verify; English wordlist extracted from reference).
- oracle op mnemonic_to_key: differential-verified against the Go
  walletapi/mnemonics — byte-identical for the simnode's wsrc seed
  (b0ef6bd5...).
- oracle op pubkey_from_seed: secret×G where G = HashToPoint("DEROG")
  (the DERO wallet base point, NOT the curve generator) and the seed is
  interpreted as a big-endian integer like Go's big.Int — byte-identical
  to the on-chain wsrc registration (09d704fe...).

The Rust wallet now derives the exact keys of the simnode's source
wallet from its recovery words — the keys that hold the on-chain
balance, ready for the transfer builder.
